## Overview

Quietmood is a powerful SaaS platform that helps you organize, manage, and collaborate on project documentation with ease. You can create structured docs, embed code snippets, and share knowledge across your team without the hassle of traditional wikis or scattered files. It supports real-time editing, version history, and powerful search to keep your documentation always up-to-date and accessible.

Whether you manage software projects, internal knowledge bases, or client deliverables, Quietmood streamlines your workflow and boosts productivity.

## Quick Start

Get up and running with Quietmood in minutes. Follow these steps to create your first documentation space.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Visit `https://app.quietmood.com` and create a free account using your email or Google login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Workspace" icon="folder-plus">
    Start a new workspace for your project. Name it something like `MyProjectDocs`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your First Page" icon="plus">
    Click "New Page" and use the MDX editor to add content, headings, and components.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Collaborators" icon="mail">
    Share your workspace link or add team emails to enable collaborative editing.
  </Step>
</Steps>
