---
title: Cannot Invite Team Members
description: Fix problems inviting users to Quietmood.
---

## Issue

- Invites are not received
- Team member can't join project

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check email address">
    Confirm the invitee's email is correct and not misspelled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend invite">
    Click "Resend" from your project members page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask to check spam">
    Sometimes invites go to spam or junk.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
Still not working? Contact support for further help.
</Callout>
