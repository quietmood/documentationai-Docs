---
title: Creating a New Project
description: How to create a documentation project in Quietmood.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Dashboard">
    Login and click on "Dashboard" in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create project">
    Click "New Project" and enter a name, e.g., `Company Docs`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start documenting">
    Open your project and click "Add New Page". Begin writing!
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Invite teammates via the Members tab anytime.
</Callout>
