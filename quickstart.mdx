## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info" title="Before You Begin">
Ensure you have:
- A valid email address for registration
- A modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- Optional: Existing project documents ready to upload (PDF, Markdown, images)
</Callout>

No software installation required—Quietmood runs entirely in your browser.

## Sign Up and Log In

Create your Quietmood account in seconds.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit Quietmood" icon="globe">
    Navigate to [https://app.quietmood.it](https://app.quietmood.it) and click **Sign Up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details" icon="user">
    Provide your email, create a password, and complete the CAPTCHA.
    
    <Callout kind="tip">
      Use a strong password with at least 12 characters.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for the verification link and click it to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">
    Return to the login page, enter your credentials, and access your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create Your First Documentation Space

Organize your projects with dedicated spaces.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Quick Create" icon="plus">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="New Space" icon="folder-plus">
        From the dashboard, click **New Documentation Space**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Name It" icon="edit-3">
        Enter a name like `My Project Docs` and select a color (#2575FC for Quietmood blue).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save" icon="check-circle">
        Click **Create**—your space is ready!
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Advanced Setup" icon="settings">
    Customize permissions and integrations during creation for teams.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Upload Your First Documents

Add content to your new space.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drag and Drop" icon="upload">
    Open your space and drag files directly into the editor area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Markdown Example" icon="code">
    Create a new MDX file and paste sample content:
    
    <CodeGroup tabs="Markdown,JSON">
    ````markdown
    # Welcome to Quietmood
    
    This is your first document.
    
    - Organize projects
    - Collaborate easily
    ````
    
    ````json
    {
      "title": "Config",
      "spaces": ["My Project Docs"]
    }
    ````
    </CodeGroup>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish" icon="eye">
    Click **Publish** to make it live and shareable.
  </Step>
</Steps>
